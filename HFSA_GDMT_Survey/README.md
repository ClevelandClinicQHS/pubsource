# HFSA GDMT Survey

An evaluation of the impact of a standardized educational module, encompassing GDMT indications, dosing, contraindications, monitoring, and side effects, on prescribers’ attitudes toward, and comfort with, GDMT prescribing.

# Publication

Chapman B, Albert NM, Zajichek A, Steinel E, Baran D, Svensson LG, Cho L, Kapadia S, Hohman J, Rothman R, Estep JD, Pavlic G, Vest A, Starling RC, Martyn T. [A Heart Failure Society of America-Developed Educational Module Improves Clinician Comfort With Initiation and Optimization of Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy](https://onlinejcf.com/article/S1071-9164(25)00300-8/fulltext). J Card Fail. 2025 Jul 19:S1071-9164(25)00300-8. doi: 10.1016/j.cardfail.2025.06.012. PMID: 40691911.
